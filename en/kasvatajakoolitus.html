Breeder's course online — autumn 2026

In autumn 2026, MTÜ Happy Dog, in cooperation with the Estonian Kennel Union, will run a breeder's course in Estonian. The course takes place online.

  • Would you like to have your own kennel name in the future?
  • Thinking that one day you could become a dog show judge?
  • Interested in pedigree dog breeding?

If you answer yes to at least one of the above, you are welcome to the breeder's course!

About the course

In autumn 2026, MTÜ Happy Dog, in cooperation with the Estonian Kennel Union, organises an EKL-recognised breeder’s course in Estonian. The course is held as online lectures! Each participant must have access to a computer (or smart device) and an internet connection. The course is carried out according to the EKL breeder’s course programme and guidelines (updated 13.11.2025). Please note that the lectures are held in Estonian.

(*) Dog nutrition (practical) takes place in groups of up to 20. The number of groups and practical session times will be determined at the end of registration. The practical lasts about 2 hours and takes place on a weekday evening at 18.00–20.00 online.

NB! The organiser may make changes to lecture times and lecturers if absolutely necessary. Participants will be informed of any changes by e-mail. Lecture end times are approximate; the exact end time depends on the length of breaks and the questions asked. The timing and length of breaks are agreed with the lecturer at the start of each lecture.

Registration and fees

  • Breeder's course€195
  • Single lecture€40
  • Retest*€40

(*) According to the breeder's course programme and guidelines, the retest can be taken once.

Registration for the course is only via the Fienta portal. Payment is possible by bank transfer, bank link or card. Registration is open until 18.10.2026; registration for single lectures closes 3 days before the lecture or once places are filled.

Important information!
Once you have registered for the course, participation cannot be cancelled. Lectures cannot be watched afterwards! To pass the course (and receive a certificate), you must attend the lectures, submit homework on time and pass the final test. Those attending single lectures (even if all lectures are bought separately) cannot take the final test. The course takes place if at least 20 participants have registered. With fewer participants the course will not take place and fees will be refunded in full. This information will be provided by 19.10.2026 at the latest.
